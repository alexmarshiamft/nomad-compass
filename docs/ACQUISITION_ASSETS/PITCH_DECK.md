---

## 1. Problem Statement
Remote workers, freelancers, and tech founders struggle with:
1. **Tax Uncertainty**: Fear of double-taxation and legal residency penalties across borders.
2. **Scattered Information**: Cost-of-living data is outdated, Reddit threads are anecdotal, and government tax guides are written in dense legal jargon.
3. **No Dynamic Tax Calculator**: Existing tools (Nomad List) focus on generic lifestyle perks rather than exact net disposable income after local tax regimes.

---

## 2. Product Solution & Architecture
Nomad Compass is a turnkey fullstack monorepo:
- **100 Curated Metropolitan Cities**: From Lisbon and Dubai to Taipei, Fukuoka, and Melbourne.
- **Double-Taxation Engine**: Simulates net take-home pay based on origin country treaties and territorial tax laws (e.g. Beckham Law, DNV 0%, Regime Forfettario).
- **Interactive Geospatial Visualizer**: Dynamic Leaflet mapping displaying monthly rent, safety, and internet speed pins.
- **AI Relocation Concierge**: Grounded Google Gemini 2.5 assistant with tool-calling capabilities that drafts customized relocation checklists.
- **Monetization Engine**: Integrated Stripe subscription paywall ($79/year Nomad Pro).
