# Nomad Compass — 3-Minute Product Walkthrough & Video Script

## Video Metadata
- **Duration**: 3 Minutes (180 Seconds)
- **Format**: Screen Share Demo + Founder Voiceover
- **Audience**: Acquire.com Buyers, MicroAcquire Investors, Tech Operators

---

## Timed Video Script

### 0:00 – 0:30: The Hook & Interactive Global Map
- **Screen**: Fullscreen browser display of Nomad Compass. 100 city markers populate the dark-mode Leaflet map with interactive price and climate tags.
- **Voiceover**: *"Finding the ideal country to live and work remotely isn't just about finding pretty beaches — it's about optimizing your taxes, cost of living, and legal runway. Welcome to Nomad Compass, the world's most sophisticated relocation and tax intelligence platform for remote professionals."*
- **Action**: Zooms into Europe, clicks Lisbon pin, then clicks Dubai.

### 0:30 – 1:15: Live Tax & Disposable Income Simulation
- **Screen**: User inputs $120,000 USD gross annual salary with US remote employer. Side-by-side comparison table dynamically calculates taxes across Lisbon (D8 visa), Spain (Beckham Law), and Dubai (0% tax).
- **Voiceover**: *"Watch what happens when we calculate net take-home pay. While standard Portuguese tax brackets reach 48%, Nomad Compass factors in double-taxation treaties and special regimes. In Spain, the Beckham Law flat 24% rate saves you over $18,000 annually. In Dubai, your total tax is zero. Every calculation itemizes monthly rent, utilities, food, and high-speed fiber."*
- **Action**: Adjusts rent and income sliders, showing real-time recalculated disposable income.

### 1:15 – 2:05: Gemini AI Tool-Calling Relocation Concierge
- **Screen**: Clicks the "AI Relocation Assistant" chat drawer.
- User prompt: *"Compare moving to Fukuoka vs. Valencia for a single software engineer earning $90k/yr who needs high-speed internet and quiet cafes."*
- **Voiceover**: *"Built directly on Google Gemini 2.5 Flash with structured function calling, the AI accesses verified city parameters and outputs an executive comparison with visa steps, banking requirements, and move-in cost estimates."*
- **Action**: AI streams back formatted response with clickable city comparison pills and 4-step relocation roadmap.

### 2:05 – 2:35: Stripe Monetization & Pro Paywall
- **Screen**: User clicks "Export Executive PDF Relocation Dossier". The Stripe Nomad Pro checkout modal appears ($79/year).
- **Voiceover**: *"Monetization is ready out of the box. Users unlock unlimited AI consultations, detailed double-taxation treaty analysis, and exportable PDF dossiers through our integrated Stripe billing engine."*
- **Action**: Demonstrates seamless Stripe checkout session generation.

### 2:35 – 3:00: Technology Stack & Handover Summary
- **Screen**: Code editor showing monorepo structure: React 19, Express 5, OpenAPI contracts, 100-city Drizzle schema, Docker, and Render/Railway manifests.
- **Voiceover**: *"Built cleanly with React 19, TypeScript, and Express 5, Nomad Compass is fully documented, tested, and turnkey ready for production hosting. All brand assets, domain configs, and codebases transfer cleanly on Acquire.com. Thanks for watching."*
